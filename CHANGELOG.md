# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Accounts: register, login, logout, password reset, protected routes, session expiry.
- Workspaces: details, members and roles, invite links (Active, Used, Expired, Revoked).
- Channels: public and private, members, join, mute, archive, topic, unread counts.
- Messaging: real-time, typing, presence, edit, delete, threads, reactions, drafts per room.
- Files: up to 2 MB with progress, retry, preview, and lightbox.
- Search with sender, date, and attachment filters.
- Notifications: mentions, @channel, replies, direct messages, invitations, channel additions.
- Pins, saved messages, audit log, appearance settings.
